The pipeline

An agent has an idea. Four things happen before it becomes a trade.

Agents produce signals. They cannot execute, cannot reach the wallet, cannot move value. Everything they suggest walks the same road, and every stage keeps a record of what it decided and why.

Security is a gate, not a score

A live mint authority means the deployer can print supply and dilute you to nothing. A live freeze authority means you can buy and never sell. Tokens carrying either are refused outright, whatever the rest of their numbers look like.

Missing data is not good news

When the chain cannot be read, the checks did not pass, they simply did not run. The gate fails closed and refuses the trade rather than assuming the best.

Every score explains itself

Ten dimensions, weights you set per agent, and a plain sentence behind each one. Any trade in the history can be traced back to the numbers that caused it.

Four buckets

One wallet, four budgets that cannot borrow from each other.

Each strategy spends its own allocation and nothing else. A bad week for one is a bad week for one.

Copy Trading30%

Follows wallets with a record worth copying, and rates that record before it copies anything.

Hot Trading30%

Scans every three minutes for unusual volume and real momentum. Most scans end with nothing, which is the point.

Hidden Gems25%

Looks for growing holders and growing volume on tokens the market has not repriced yet.

Low Market Cap15%

The smallest budget for the largest risk. Stricter security, stricter liquidity, tighter stops.

Change the split whenever you like. The console refuses anything that does not total 100, and only uninvested cash moves.

The five

Four of them trade. One reads the room. None of them can overrule the gate.

01

Copy Trade

Tracks wallets, scores them on win rate, average return, drawdown and consistency, and flags the ones whose timing looks like a bot. A leader you cannot realistically follow is worth nothing.

02

Hidden Gem

Market cap, liquidity, volume growth, holder growth, wallet spread, token age, smart money. Every gem it surfaces says why it was picked and what worried it.

03

Hot Trade

Runs every three minutes. Volume spikes measured against the token's own baseline, not against other tokens. It refuses to chase something that has already run.

04

Small Market Cap

The strictest of the four. It will not trade a token whose holder distribution it cannot see, because that is the exact situation it exists to avoid.

05

Social Intelligence

Mentions, sentiment, trend and source quality, plus a spam score whose whole job is to stop bought hype reading as demand. It scores tokens for the other four and never trades itself.

And the gate

Token Security

Contract authorities, taxes, transfer limits, liquidity locks, holder concentration, wash trading, copycat symbols. It holds the blacklist and it has a veto over all five.

It is not on a schedule like the others. It runs before every single trade, and again inside the pipeline, because time passes between an agent liking a token and the money actually moving.

Safety

The boring parts are the ones that matter.

Paper is the default

Live trading needs two independent switches: the configuration set to live, and a confirmation phrase in your environment file. Either one alone keeps it on paper.

Nothing succeeds until it is confirmed

A live trade is not reported as filled until the transaction is confirmed on chain. A submitted-but-unconfirmed send opens no position.

Your keys stay yours

The private key is read from your environment, held in memory, and never written to the database, the logs, the API or the screen.

A kill switch that does one thing

It stops new positions instantly. It deliberately does not force-sell, because a panic button that dumps into a thin market can cost more than the thing it was pressed for.

Limits that shrink before they refuse

Position size, bucket exposure, total exposure, daily loss, single token concentration, pool share. An oversized trade gets cut down rather than thrown away.

It runs on your machine

A local database, a local API bound to localhost, and free data tiers. Nothing is sent anywhere you did not configure.

Start it in paper mode and watch a week go by.
